# The Invisible Influence Detector

**Are your decisions really your own?**

## About the Project

The Invisible Influence Detector is a C-based project that explores how the people and information around us can affect the decisions we make in everyday life.

We often think that our choices are completely independent, but factors like peer pressure, popularity, FOMO and the influence of authority can sometimes change our thinking without us even noticing.

This project lets users experience such situations through simple interactive simulations. It records their choices before and after introducing an external influence, allowing them to compare their decisions and understand what might have affected them.

## Features

- **Social Pressure Simulator:** Shows how friends and group opinions can influence personal choices.
- **FOMO Simulator:** Demonstrates how limited-time offers and scarcity messages can push people into making quick decisions.
- **Popularity Influence:** Explores how ratings, majority opinions and popular choices affect our judgement.
- **Authority & Persuasion:** Shows how the same information can seem more believable when presented by someone considered an expert.
- **Repeated Exposure:** Demonstrates how seeing the same claim multiple times can make it feel more familiar and believable.
- **Decision Awareness Report:** Displays completed scenarios, changed decisions and category-wise results.
- **Independent Thinking Checklist:** Includes six questions that encourage users to reflect on their decisions.
- **Previous Results:** Allows users to view their earlier choices and compare them.

## How Does It Work?

1. Select a simulation from the main menu.
2. Read the given situation and choose between two options.
3. The program introduces a new piece of information or an external influence.
4. Make your decision again.
5. The program compares your initial and final choices.
6. A short explanation highlights the influence involved.
7. Your results are saved and can be viewed later.

## Technologies Used

- **Language:** C
- **Concepts Used:** Functions, Structures, Arrays, Loops, Conditional Statements and Switch Cases.
- **File Handling:** To save and retrieve previous results.

## How Results Are Stored

The program creates a text file called `influence_records.txt` to store completed scenarios.

Whenever a user finishes a scenario, their initial choice, final choice and other related details are saved. These records are loaded again when the program is run, so previous results are not lost when it closes.
